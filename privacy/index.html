Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Chord Solo Guitar runs a small private tool that uses Google's YouTube APIs. It reads statistics and analytics for one YouTube channel only, the Chord Solo Guitar channel owned by Matt Jones, for his own use in planning videos.

What it uses

The channel owner's own video and channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers, and similar figures). It does not access anyone else's account and does not collect personal information about viewers or visitors.

What it stores and shares

Summary numbers may be saved privately for the channel owner's records. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. This site uses no cookies or tracking.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
